What we do

Restorations, repairs and everything in between.

Concrete tile, terracotta and metal roofs. From one cracked tile to a full restoration or replacement.

Full roof restorations

The complete treatment for a faded, mossy or leaking roof. A full restoration covers:

  • High-pressure clean
  • Broken tiles replaced
  • Ridge caps re-bedded and re-pointed
  • Valleys repaired or replaced
  • Sealed and re-coated in your colour
Get a restoration quote

Re-bedding & re-pointing

Cracked ridge mortar lets caps work loose and water in. Caps are re-bedded in fresh mortar and finished with new pointing.

Roof painting & sealing

Sealer and fresh top coats protect porous tiles and faded metal, and bring the colour back.

High-pressure cleaning

Years of dirt, moss and lichen stripped off. Done on its own, or as the first stage of a restoration.

Valley replacement

Rusted or worn valley irons are a common cause of leaks. The old valleys come out and new ones go in.

Broken tile replacement

Cracked and slipped tiles replaced with the closest match available, before they turn into a leak.

Roof repairs & leaks

Leaks tracked down and fixed, whether it’s a cracked tile, a gap at the ridge or a tired valley.

Whirlybirds

Whirlybird vents fitted to let hot, damp air out of the roof space.

Gutter & drain cleaning

Leaves and silt cleared so rain runs off the roof and away from the house.

How a restoration runs

From first look to final coat.

A full restoration runs in five stages. Each one sets up the next.

  1. 01

    Inspect and quote

    A look over the whole roof, then a free, no-obligation quote that sets out the work.

  2. 02

    Clean

    High-pressure cleaning strips dirt, moss and lichen so repairs and coatings go onto a sound surface.

  3. 03

    Repair

    Broken tiles replaced, valleys fixed, and ridge caps re-bedded and re-pointed.

  4. 04

    Seal and coat

    A sealer goes on first, then the top coats in the colour you’ve picked.

  5. 05

    Finished

    Your roof is sealed, coated and ready for the next storm season.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How do I know my roof needs restoring?

Common signs are faded or patchy colour, moss and lichen, cracked or crumbling mortar on the ridge caps, broken or slipped tiles, rust on metal, and water stains on the ceiling after rain. If you can see a few of these, book a free inspection.

What’s included in a full restoration?

A full restoration usually means a high-pressure clean, replacing broken tiles, re-bedding and re-pointing the ridge caps, any valley repairs, then sealing and re-coating. Your quote spells out exactly what your roof needs.

Can you just fix a leak?

Yes. Not every roof needs a full restoration. We do repairs on their own, including broken tiles, leaks, valleys and ridge capping.

Do you work on metal roofs?

Yes. We work on concrete tile, terracotta and metal (tin) roofs.

What if my roof is past restoring?

We’ll tell you straight. If a roof is beyond a restoration, we can quote a full roof replacement instead.

How much does a restoration cost?

It depends on the size, pitch and condition of the roof. Quotes are free and there’s no obligation, so it costs nothing to find out.

How long does it take?

That depends on the size of the roof, the repairs it needs and the weather. You’ll get a time frame with your quote.

When can I call?
